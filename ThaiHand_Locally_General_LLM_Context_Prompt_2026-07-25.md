### Core business concept

Locally was conceived as a two-sided service marketplace connecting foreigners in Thailand, initially focused on Bangkok, with trusted local assistants who could help with practical tasks and local navigation.

Potential customers:

- long-stay expatriates
- digital nomads
- relocating professionals
- foreigners already living in or arriving in Bangkok
- customers facing recurring local friction

Potential assistant services discussed:

- errands
- translation
- local vendor coordination
- landlord communication
- government-office guidance
- document pickup or delivery
- hospital or clinic navigation
- personal shopping
- local recommendations
- in-person concierge-style help
- case-specific virtual research or coordination

Some services, especially regulated, high-risk, medical, immigration, transport, financial, or physical-access tasks, required legal and operational review before launch.

### Strategic principle

The strongest internal framing was:

> **Trust gap, not information gap.**

Meaning:

- Customers can already search online or ask an AI for information.
- The more valuable problem is finding a reliable, accountable person who can act locally and complete a task.

The strongest customer-facing slogan discussed and accepted was:

> **Local help you can trust.**

### Positioning caution

The source materials alternated between:

- in-person local help
- relocation/concierge support
- Taskrabbit-style task fulfillment
- virtual assistance as an initial safer service layer

Do not claim the final service model was settled. The most defensible conclusion is that the project was still validating its initial service scope.

## 19. Prompt-design rationale

This file is structured for broad LLM compatibility:

- instructions appear before long context
- source authority is explicit
- facts, disputes, and unknowns are separated
- stable context is grouped into labeled sections
- exact output behavior is defined
- sensitive data is excluded
- the current relationship status appears prominently
- duplicate narrative has been compressed
- reusable task rules are included

Prompting is iterative. If an LLM misses a detail, point it to the relevant numbered section and add the specific current task at the end.
